## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I reset my password?**
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and follow the emailed reset link.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to your Profile tab and tap "Delete Account" — this permanently deletes your account and personal data. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) for details.
